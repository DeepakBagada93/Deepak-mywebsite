## 💎 3. Content Architecture Standards: Every Dispatch Must Have

To guarantee rank #1 positions and high-converting readers:

1. **Title Length & Format**: Strictly **50–60 characters**. Front-load keyword in the first 25 characters. **ZERO SQUARE BRACKETS (`[ ]`)**. Use clean high-converting punctuation (`:`, `—`, `|`, `&`, `₹`, `%`).
2. **Meta Description**: Strictly **145–158 characters**. Active verb + primary search query + concrete metric + CTA hook.
3. **Word Count**: **1,400 to 2,000+ words** of dense, senior practitioner engineering depth.
4. **The AEO Direct Answer Block**: 40–60 word declarative answer in the opening paragraph for instant citation in Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT, and Google AI Overviews.
5. **Key Architecture Takeaways**: 4-bullet executive TL;DR box.
6. **Visual Architecture Diagram**: Clean Mermaid flowchart (`flowchart TD` or `sequenceDiagram`) to drop bounce rate below 40%.
7. **Runnable Production Code**: Complete multi-file implementations with explicit language tags and filenames (`# app/agents/graph.py`).
8. **Structured Markdown Comparison Matrix**: 3–6 columns, 4–8 rows featuring real numbers (P95 latency, memory, ₹ cost).
9. **Mandatory Product Monetization Box**: Contextual bridge to one of Deepak's 27 digital products (`/products/*`) or client advisory (`/#contact`).
10. **Zero AI Slop**: Strict audit rejection for banned buzzwords ("delve", "game-changer", "tapestry", "revolutionize").
