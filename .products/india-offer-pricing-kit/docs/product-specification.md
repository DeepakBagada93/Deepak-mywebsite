# Product Specification — India Offer & Pricing Kit

Version 1.0.0 · 2026-10-20

## 1. Promise

From "I do not know what to charge" to a GST-aware price ladder, a break-even ad budget and an offer page that passes a trust audit — in one working session.

## 2. Transformation

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Price copied from a US product | Price derived from floor net and Indian anchors |
| "₹1,499 + GST" at checkout | One GST-inclusive price with a visible breakup |
| Guesswork on ad spend | Break-even CPA and ROAS from real arithmetic |
| Page with no refund, contact or UPI | Trust block that Indian buyers expect |
| Fake countdown and invented anchor | Real deadline and a defensible reference price |
| Price changed weekly | One-variable test with a 7-day rule |

## 3. Target buyer

Primary: Indian founder or creator launching a digital product between ₹499 and ₹9,999.
Secondary: agency or consultant pricing client offers, courses and retainers.
Not for: enterprises needing a CFO model, or sellers outside India.

## 4. Deliverables

| # | Artifact | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `SKILL.md` | 7 stages, economics schema, page blocks, scoring rubric, constraints |
| 2 | Pricing engine | GST + gateway maths, floor net, psychological ladder, launch/bundle, ad economics |
| 3 | Offer audit | 15+ checks including GST display, GSTIN, refund, contact, UPI, dark patterns, claims |
| 4 | Page copy framework | Headline, proof, inclusions, price block, FAQ, footer |
| 5 | Examples | Single product, bundle, 100/100 page, 0/100 page, expected reports |
| 6 | Tests | Structural + functional, zero dependencies |
| 7 | Tutorial | Quickstart, 4 installs, 4 recipes, troubleshooting, FAQ |
| 8 | Store listing | Hook, proof, inclusions, India pricing, guarantee, FAQ |

## 5. Pricing

| Tier | Price | Seats | Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo | ₹999 | 1 | Skill, scripts, examples, tutorial, v1.x updates |
| Pro | ₹2,499 | 5 | Everything + priority email support |
| Agency | ₹4,999 | Unlimited | Everything + client pricing license |

Launch offer: Solo at ₹699 for the first 100 buyers.
Bundle: with Ad Creative Lab and Content Repurpose Engine at ₹3,499 regular, ₹2,999 launch.

Rationale: lowest entry of the three because the buyer is pre-revenue or early. The agency tier carries the value for consultants who price client offers repeatedly.

## 6. Success metrics

- Buyer produces a defensible ladder in under 10 minutes.
- Buyer can state net revenue and break-even CPA without a spreadsheet.
- Offer page passes 90+ before paid traffic.
- Refund rate below 5%.

## 7. Non-goals

- GST filing, invoicing or accounting.
- Legal advice on claims or contracts.
- Storefront checkout implementation.
- Guaranteed conversion or revenue.
