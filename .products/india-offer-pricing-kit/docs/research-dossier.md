# Market Research Dossier — India Offer & Pricing Kit

Date: 2026-10-20

## 1. Why India pricing is its own problem

US pricing advice does not transfer. Four local mechanics change the number:

| Mechanic | Effect on price |
| --- | --- |
| GST at 18% on digital services | A ₹1,499 display nets ₹1,270 before gateway fees, not ₹1,499 |
| Payment gateway at ~2% + 18% GST on the fee | Effective ~2.36% of the pre-GST value |
| Refunds and support | 3–5% refunds plus ₹50–₹150 of support per sale is normal |
| Price anchors | Indian buyers compare against ₹499 courses and free YouTube, not $49 SaaS |

Sources: CBIC (GST), Razorpay pricing page (2% domestic cards), Playto, "How to Sell Digital Products in India 2026" (entry products ₹499–₹4,999; mid-tier ₹5,000–₹50,000).
- https://www.cbic.gov.in
- https://razorpay.com/pricing/
- https://www.playto.so/blogs/how-to-sell-digital-products-india-2026

## 2. Compliance surface

| Rule | What it means for a sales page |
| --- | --- |
| CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns (2023) | False urgency, drip pricing, basket sneaking and disguised ads are prohibited patterns |
| ASCI Code | Superlatives and guarantees need substantiation |
| Consumer Protection Act 2019 | Misleading claims and unfair trade practices create liability |
| RBI e-mandate rules | Recurring charges need explicit consent, pre-debit notification and a cancellation path |
| GST invoice rules | Registered sellers must show GSTIN and issue a tax invoice |

Sources: CCPA (ccpa.gov.in), ASCI (ascionline.in), RBI (rbi.org.in), CBIC (cbic.gov.in).

## 3. What existing tools miss

| Alternative | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| US pricing calculators | No GST, no UPI, no Indian anchor psychology |
| Agency pricing decks | One-off advice, no repeatable audit |
| Store checkout settings | Compute tax, not whether the price is viable |
| Prompt packs | No arithmetic, no compliance checks |

The gap is a **calculator plus an audit**: the number and the page that has to sell it.

## 4. Buyer and willingness to pay

| Segment | Pain | Willingness to pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo creator launching a first digital product | Afraid to underprice, no cost model | ₹499–₹999 |
| D2C brand / edtech founder | GST + gateway + refunds erode margin | ₹1,499–₹2,499 |
| Agency pricing client offers | Inconsistent ladders, weak pages | ₹4,999–₹7,999 |
| Course creator at ₹5,000+ | Needs payment plans and trust signals | ₹2,499–₹4,999 |

India digital-product guidance supports a ₹999 entry and a ₹4,999 agency tier. The bundle with the other two skills is priced at ₹3,499 regular / ₹2,999 launch.

## 5. Evidence limits

- GST treatment depends on registration, turnover and the nature of supply; a CA must confirm.
- Gateway rates are negotiated and change; the default 2% is a planning assumption.
- No live conversion data was used. The 7-day measurement plan exists because the right price is discovered, then defended.
- Dark-pattern screening is text matching on the supplied page fields, not a legal opinion.
