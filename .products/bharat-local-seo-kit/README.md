## ⚡ What is Bharat Local SEO Kit?

Across India, over 70 million MSMEs—from dental clinics and CA firms to retail showrooms and restaurants—rely on Google Maps for local footfall. Yet, most businesses struggle to rank in the coveted **Google 3-Pack** or maintain consistent updates.

The **Bharat Local SEO Kit** provides:
1. **Local NAP & Category Optimization**: Standardizes citations and fixes algorithm-penalizing discrepancies.
2. **Hyper-Local Indian Schema Generator**: Produces validated JSON-LD schema featuring local landmarks, opening hours, and geo-coordinates.
3. **52-Week Bharat Festive Post Calendar**: Ready-to-publish Google Business Profile updates for Diwali, Eid, Navratri, Holi, Pongal, and wedding seasons.
4. **Sentiment-Aware Multilingual Review Responder**: Crafts diplomatic, conversion-oriented replies in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.
5. **Agency Retainer Pitch Engine**: Enables digital freelancers and agencies to sign local clients on ₹10,000–₹25,000/month retainers.

1. Load `skills/bharat-local-seo-kit/` into Google Antigravity, Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex.

   ```markdown

   - Business: Dr. Verma Multi-Speciality Dental Clinic
   - Locality: Indiranagar 100ft Road, Bengaluru (Near Metro Station)
   - Category: Cosmetic Dentist & Implant Specialist
   - Goal: Generate Festive Diwali Campaign & Review Response for a 1-star billing complaint.
   ```
3. Copy the generated schema directly into your website header and publish the festive update to your Google Business Profile!
