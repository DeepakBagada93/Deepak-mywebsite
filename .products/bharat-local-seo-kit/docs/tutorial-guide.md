# Customer Tutorial & Quickstart Guide: Bharat Local SEO Kit

Turn your Google Business Profile into a 24/7 customer magnet in under 3 minutes.

---

## ⚡ 3-Minute Quickstart

### Step 1: Install the Skill
Add `skills/bharat-local-seo-kit/` to your workspace skills directory in Antigravity or Claude Code.

### Step 2: Run the Optimization Engine
Paste this command into your AI assistant:

```markdown
Run Bharat Local SEO Kit:
- Business Name: Shanti Diagnostic & Pathology Laboratory
- City & Locality: Gomti Nagar, Lucknow (Near Manoj Pandey Chauraha)
- Services: Full Body Health Checkups, Blood Tests, Thyroid Profiles, Home Sample Collection
- Output Required:
  1. Google 3-Pack Keyword Matrix
  2. LocalBusiness JSON-LD Schema
  3. Navratri / Festive Health Package Post
  4. Response for a 5-star review praising fast home collection
  5. Response for a 1-star review complaining about report delay
```

### Step 3: Copy & Deploy
1. **Schema**: Paste the generated JSON-LD script into your website header.
2. **GBP Post**: Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com), click "Add update", paste the post and add your image.
3. **Reviews**: Paste the empathetic review replies directly into Google Maps Manager.
