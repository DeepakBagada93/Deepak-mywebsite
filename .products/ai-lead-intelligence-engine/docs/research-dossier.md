## 1. Executive Summary & Market Problem

In B2B sales, speed-to-lead is the single highest predictor of conversion. Harvard Business Review studies reveal that companies contacting inbound leads within 5 minutes are **21 times more likely to qualify them** than those waiting 30 minutes.

Yet, 78% of B2B companies take more than 24 hours to review inbound leads because:
1. Sales reps manually review form entries and cross-reference LinkedIn or Clearbit.
2. Form fields are sparse (just name and email), requiring tedious manual research before outreach.
3. High-ticket custom agency solutions (like Clearbit + 6sense + custom code) cost **$12,000 to $36,000/year**.

`ai-lead-intelligence-engine` bridges this gap: a turnkey, self-hostable automation blueprint that takes any raw form lead, enriches corporate domain signals, prompts GPT-4o for scoring (0–100) and an instant personalized email icebreaker, updates HubSpot/Salesforce, and pings the on-duty sales rep in Slack in under 3 seconds.
