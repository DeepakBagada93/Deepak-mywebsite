## 🚀 Overview

Are you tired of your AI coding assistant (Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot) hallucinating deprecated Next.js 13 patterns in your Next.js 15 app, using obsolete Tailwind syntax, or generating invalid database queries?

The **Codebase Context Engine** automatically crawls your repository, resolves your exact dependency versions, and synthesizes token-dense `.cursorrules` and `CLAUDE.md` files.
