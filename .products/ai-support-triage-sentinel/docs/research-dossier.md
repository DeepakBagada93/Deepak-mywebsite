## 1. Market Opportunity & Problem Validation

Customer support teams face a recurring dilemma:
1. **The Churn Vulnerability:** An enterprise customer who submits an urgent billing failure or database outage ticket waiting 4+ hours in a general queue will churn. Studies show that 67% of customer churn is preventable if resolution is initiated within the first 15 minutes.
2. **Support Agent Burnout:** 40% to 60% of tier-1 support tickets are repetitive inquiries (password resets, invoice copies, basic status checks) that clog queues and slow down engineering escalations.
3. **Expensive Native AI Add-ons:** Zendesk AI and Intercom Fin cost an additional **$50 to $100 per agent/month** plus per-resolution fees ($0.99 per AI answer), leading to soaring recurring bills.

`ai-support-triage-sentinel` solves this with an owned, self-hostable workflow that operates across any helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, or custom email) to instantly triage tickets, evaluate churn danger, draft high-empathy solutions, and immediately notify senior support engineers in Slack.
