## 4. Checkout & Payment Infrastructure Setup

### For Indian Buyers (Zero Drop-Off Flow):
1. **Gateway:** Cashfree Payments or Razorpay Payment Links / Pages.
2. **Payment Methods:**
   - **UPI Intent / Dynamic QR Code** (Instant scan via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
   - RuPay & Visa/Mastercard Debit Cards.
   - Net Banking (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis).
3. **Fulfillment:** Webhook instantly emails the customer download ZIP and sends an instant WhatsApp confirmation link.

### For US & International Buyers:
1. **Gateway:** Lemon Squeezy (acts as Merchant of Record, handling EU VAT and US state sales tax) or Stripe Checkout.
2. **Payment Methods:** Credit Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
3. **Fulfillment:** Instant download redirect on checkout completion + order confirmation email with perpetual license key.
