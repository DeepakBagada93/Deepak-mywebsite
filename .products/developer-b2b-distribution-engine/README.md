## 🚀 Overview

Are you a brilliant software developer or technical agency founder struggling to land high-paying clients?

Most developers pitch technical jargon (frameworks, tech stacks, database schemas) rather than business outcomes, sending rambling 6-paragraph cold emails that get deleted within 3 seconds.

The **Developer B2B Distribution Engine** turns your technical skills into a high-converting client acquisition machine.

It combines:
1. **Intent-Signal Scorer CLI (`scripts/intent-scraper.mjs`)**: Evaluates prospective companies based on stalled job postings, tech migrations, and UX friction.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Client AI Agent Skill (`skills/b2b-distribution-engine/SKILL.md`)**: Generates 4-sentence hyper-personalized outreach sequences, 3-minute Loom video audit scripts, and fixed-price risk-reversal proposals.
