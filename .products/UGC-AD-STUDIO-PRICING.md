## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Every D2C founder, media buyer, and performance marketing agency faces the same painful bottleneck in 2026: **Ad fatigue happens in 7 days, but producing fresh, consistent UGC creatives takes 2 weeks and costs ₹30,000–₹1,00,000.**

When teams turn to generative AI video tools (Veo 3.1, Google Flow, Kling, Runway, HeyGen), they hit five fatal failure modes that burn their ad spend:
1. **Visual Drift:** The creator's face, skin tone, clothing, and the product's packaging shift erratically between Scene 1 and Scene 4.
2. **Hook Truncation & Pacing Drag:** Hooks exceed 14 words or hit after second 3, while dialogue speeds exceed 3.5 words/second, making the ad unlistenable on mobile feeds.
3. **Unsubstantiated Claims & Ad Account Bans:** Ads contain banned trigger words ("cure", "guaranteed #1", medical promises) or miss mandatory AI-generation disclosures, triggering automated Meta and TikTok policy rejections.
4. **Single-Angle Creative Bias:** Marketers test only one angle (usually a feature pitch) and conclude "AI ads don't convert" when the audience needed an objection-reversal or a cost-of-inaction angle.
5. **Recurring SaaS Subscriptions:** Marketers pay $29–$110/month for web-based prompt wrappers with rigid token credits, proprietary watermarks, and zero compliance audits.

### The Winning Solution:
**`ugc-ad-studio`** is a **100% offline, zero-dependency, deterministic AI production skill and pre-spend CLI auditor**. You feed it a single product brief (`brief.json`), and in under 60 seconds it deterministically generates:
- **6 Psychological Ad Angles:** Cost of Inaction, Failed Alternative, Mechanism, Proof, Objection Reversal, Identity.
- **Timed 5-Beat Scripts:** Precise second-by-second UGC video scripts calibrated to 15s, 30s, or 45s runtimes.
- **Verbatim Model Prompts:** 30 video prompts (Veo 3.1, Google Flow, Kling, Runway Gen-3) and 24 clean static plates (Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney, Flux) with locked 4-part consistency tokens (Character, Product, Grade, Craft).
- **HeyGen Avatar Pack & Native Platform Captions:** Spoken presenter scripts with voice direction and platform-compliant copy (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).
- **Automated 38-Check Pre-Spend Audit CLI:** A deterministic test suite scoring the entire pack /100 and outputting `PASS`, `FIX NEEDED`, or `DO NOT SHIP` before spending a single rupee on ad platforms.
