## 2. The gap

Free skills and prompt packs are good at **writing one piece**. They are weak at the actual job:

1. **Pipeline, not a prompt.** A founder does not need one more caption; they need 30 dated pieces mapped to insights and platforms.
2. **No duplicate detection.** Cross-posting the same paragraph to four platforms is the default failure mode. Nothing in the free ecosystem catches it deterministically.
3. **No platform-native enforcement.** X 280, Instagram 2,200, LinkedIn 3,000, blog title 60, meta 155. A prompt that "keeps it short" does not enforce anything.
4. **No India language layer.** Hinglish is common in Indian D2C and creator content, and free US-centric packs silently ignore it.

## 3. Buyer and willingness to pay

| Segment | Content volume | Pain | Willingness to pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo creator / consultant | 8–20 posts/month | No time, inconsistent calendar | ₹499–₹1,499 |
| D2C brand founder | 30–60 pieces/month | Agency retainer ₹20,000+/month | ₹1,499–₹2,999 |
| Small agency | 5–20 clients | Juniors cross-post, clients notice | ₹2,999–₹5,999 |
| Content lead in a 20–100 person company | 40+ pieces/month | No system, no audit | ₹2,999–₹5,999 |

India digital-product guidance (Playto, 2026) supports ₹499–₹4,999 entry products and ₹5,000–₹50,000 mid-tier. A ₹1,299 anchor with a ₹5,999 agency tier fits the entry-to-mid band.

## 4. Competitive alternatives

| Alternative | Why buyers leave |
| --- | --- |
| Free skill repos | No calendar, no audit, no duplicate detection |
| Content agency | ₹20,000–₹60,000/month; slow feedback loop |
| Scheduling SaaS | Schedules anything, including bad copy; does not write or audit |
| Prompt packs | One-off output, no repeatable pipeline |
