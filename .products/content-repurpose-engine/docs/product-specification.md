# Product Specification — Content Repurpose Engine

Version 1.0.0 · 2026-10-20

## 1. Promise

From one source asset to a 30-day, platform-native content calendar that passes an audit before anything is scheduled.

## 2. Transformation

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| One post per week, written from scratch | 30 dated pieces from one source |
| Same paragraph pasted to four platforms | Rewritten per platform, duplicate-checked |
| No idea what to post next | Angle + insight + format assigned per day |
| Generic CTAs | One platform-appropriate CTA per piece |
| Schedule and hope | Audit score and fixes before publishing |

## 3. Target buyer

Primary: Indian D2C founder, solo creator or consultant publishing 8–30 pieces a month.
Secondary: small agency producing content for 5–20 clients.
Not for: enterprises with a dedicated editorial team and an existing CMS workflow, or buyers who want auto-publishing.

## 4. Deliverables

| # | Artifact | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `SKILL.md` | 7 stages, input contract, platform table, scoring rubric, negative constraints |
| 2 | Planner | 30 pieces, deterministic dates, platform weights, full insight coverage, no duplicate cells |
| 3 | Audit engine | Limits, CTA, hashtags, AI phrases, duplicates, SEO fields; JSON + Markdown |
| 4 | Platform library | 6 platforms, 13 formats, limits and CTA styles |
| 5 | Examples | Source brief, 100/100 pack, 0/100 flawed pack, expected reports |
| 6 | Tests | Structural + functional, zero dependencies |
| 7 | Tutorial | Quickstart, 4 installs, 4 recipes, troubleshooting, FAQ |
| 8 | Store listing | Hook, proof, inclusions, India pricing, guarantee, FAQ |

## 5. Pricing

| Tier | Price | Seats | Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo | ₹1,299 | 1 | Skill, scripts, examples, tutorial, 1 year of updates |
| Pro | ₹2,999 | 5 | Everything + priority email support |
| Agency | ₹5,999 | Unlimited | Everything + client-delivery license |

Launch offer: Solo at ₹899 for the first 100 buyers.
Bundle: with Ad Creative Lab and India Offer & Pricing Kit at ₹3,999.

Rationale: the buyer is a creator or small team, so the entry price is below the ads product. The agency tier carries the margin because client work is the highest-value use.

## 6. Success metrics

- Buyer produces a 30-day plan in under 15 minutes.
- At least 80% of planned pieces survive the audit without a critical finding.
- Buyer stops cross-posting the same body.
- Refund rate below 5%.

## 7. Non-goals

- Auto-publishing to social platforms or email tools.
- Image, video or design generation.
- Trend discovery from live platform data.
- Guaranteed reach, followers or revenue.
