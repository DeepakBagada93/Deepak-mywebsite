## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Every SEO agency, freelance consultant, and B2B marketing leader is facing the exact same client question in 2026: **"Why does ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude recommend our competitor instead of us?"**

The traditional response from agencies is either blank stares or panic:
1. **The Screenshot Trap:** Taking a single screenshot of a ChatGPT session and presenting it as an "AI SEO Audit" for $2,000. Clients quickly realize it's non-repeatable snake oil.
2. **The Cloud SaaS Tax:** Paying $99 to $499 every month for fragile web apps that scrape LLM endpoints, run out of API credits, and produce unpredictable score swings when frontier model weights change.
3. **The `llms.txt` Fallacy:** Selling clients that adding a single text file will magically grant them top citations, ignoring semantic content architecture and entity schema.
4. **Zero Actionable Fixes:** Telling clients "you need better citations" without providing the exact JSON-LD schema, answer-first extraction blocks, or robots.txt crawler unblocks.

### The Winning Solution:
**`geo-visibility-os`** is a **100% offline, zero-dependency, deterministic Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) audit and fix engine**. Point it at a website URL or a local HTML snapshot, and in under 2 seconds it deterministically generates:
- **40-Check Weighted Audit:** Scored 0–100 with clear severity findings and remediation instructions.
- **30-Prompt Multi-Engine Buyer Panel:** Five funnel stages (Awareness, Comparison, Decision, Objection, Branded) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews.
- **6 Answer-First Content Briefs:** Direct 40–60 word answer blocks, evidence requirements, schema markup, and internal link strategies.
- **Clean JSON-LD Schema Pack:** Organization, Service/Product, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, and Article schema without fabricated claims.
- **90-Day Prioritized Fix Roadmap:** 25+ concrete actions with owner assignment, impact vs. effort scoring, and verification steps.
- **10-Source Citation Strategy & Measurement Plan:** GSC generative AI tracking, Bing citation share, GA4 AI referrals, and manual test panel.
