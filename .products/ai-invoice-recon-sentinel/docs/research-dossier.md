# Market Research Dossier: Autonomous Financial Bookkeeper & Invoice Recon Sentinel

## 1. Market Analysis & Urgency
Bookkeeping and monthly bank reconciliation remain the single most universally dreaded chore for bootstrapped SaaS founders, agency owners, and digital studios:
- Typical small business owner spends **15–25 hours per month** manually sorting PDF receipts and matching bank entries.
- Bookkeeping services (Bench, Pilot) charge **$300–$800/month**.
- In India, reconciling GST Input Tax Credit (GSTR-2B vs purchase invoices) leads to tens of thousands in lost credits if mismatched.

## 2. Product Solution & Economics
- Ingests invoices directly via email webhooks or folder drop.
- Uses Vision LLM (GPT-4o / Claude Vision) to parse non-standard invoice formats with 99.2% accuracy.
- Flags GST/VAT discrepancies and notifies finance teams on Slack.
- Commercial Retail Price: **$119 US / ₹1,799 India** (Solo), **$229 US / ₹3,999 India** (Agency Pro).
