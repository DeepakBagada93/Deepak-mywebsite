## 1. Market Opportunity & Problem Validation

Organic search remains the #1 highest-ROI acquisition channel for software and service companies. However, Google's Helpful Content System updates strictly penalize synthetic, shallow AI articles.

Modern marketing teams face two extremes:
1. **Expensive Human Writers:** Paying $250 to $600 per technical article limits velocity to 2–4 articles per month.
2. **Cheap AI Spam Tools (Jasper, Copy.ai):** Generate robotic, fluff-filled listicles that get zero search impressions and risk domain penalties.

`ai-content-factory-pipeline` provides a middle ground: an automated, multi-agent pipeline that enforces technical rigor, schema markup, and anti-AI-fluff patterns while publishing directly into WordPress, Webflow, or Ghost with 1-click human editorial review in Slack.
