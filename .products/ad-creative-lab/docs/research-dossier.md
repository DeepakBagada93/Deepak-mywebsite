## 2. The gap this product fills

The market is crowded with **generation** skills ("write me 10 hooks") and **account-audit** skills that require live API access. Three gaps remain:

1. **No deterministic creative audit.** Existing skills ask a model to judge copy. Buyers cannot tell whether a score is reproducible. Ad Creative Lab ships a rules engine: same input, same score, every time.
2. **No India-first compliance layer.** Free US-centric packs do not check ASCI-style claim classes, rupee price consistency, Hinglish labelling, or the 125-character Meta cut.
3. **No budget math.** Most packs stop at copy. Founders still do not know their break-even CPA or whether ₹30,000 can produce a readable test. The test-matrix planner closes that loop.

## 5. Evidence limits

- Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center were not scraped in this build; no claim of "ads running 30+ days" is made.
- No live account data was used. Projections in the planner are explicitly labelled planning inputs.
- Free-skill supply is a real threat. The paid value is the audit engine, the test harness, India compliance and the budget model.
