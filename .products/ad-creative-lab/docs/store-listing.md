## The problem

You have a product, a price and a budget. What you do not have is a way to tell whether the ad is the problem or the audience is.

So you boost a post. You get 40 clicks. You conclude ads do not work.

Meanwhile the actual issues are boring: a 30-word first line, an unprovable claim, a headline that gets truncated, and a ₹5,000 budget spread across nine audiences.

## The solution

One skill. Six stages. A score you can defend.

| You get | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Deterministic audit** | 20+ checks for claims, limits, hook strength, price consistency, duplicates and UGC pacing. Same input, same score. |
| **Six-angle rebuild** | Cost of inaction, failed alternative, mechanism, proof, objection reversal, identity. |

| **India compliance pass** | ASCI-style claim classes, rupee price consistency, Meta and Google limits, Hinglish labelling. |
