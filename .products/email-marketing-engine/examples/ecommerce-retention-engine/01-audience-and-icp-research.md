# 01. Audience, ICP & Competitor Email Research

**Brand / Product**: Lumina Glow (Clean Clinical Skincare)  
**Target Category**: D2C E-Commerce & Retail Wholesalers  
**Date**: October 2026

---

## 1. Audience & Customer Persona

### D2C Consumers (B2C Cohort):
- **Demographics**: Women aged 25–45, urban/suburban, high disposable income ($85k+), eco-conscious.
- **Core Motivation**: Effective, non-toxic anti-aging & barrier repair without irritating sensitive skin.
- **Pain Points**: Breakouts from heavy creams, false marketing claims from synthetic brands, confusing multi-step routines.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $74.00.

### Boutique Retail Buyers (B2B Wholesale Outreach):
- **Target**: Independent clean beauty boutique owners, spa directors, dermatology clinics.
- **Pain Points**: Low wholesale margins from mega-brands, inventory minimums, lack of promotional support.

---

## 2. Competitor Email Teardowns & Market White Space

- **Competitors**: Drunk Elephant, Youth to the People, Biossance.
- **Common Weaknesses**: Over-reliance on generic 15% discount blasts, heavy promotional images with zero educational copy, spam folder issues during Black Friday/Cyber Monday.
- **Our Positioning**: Ingredient transparency, dermatological clinical proof, routine-building guides, and personalized skin quiz onboarding.
