**Campaign**: Lumina Glow B2B Retailer & Boutique Partnership  
**Target**: Independent Clean Beauty Retail Buyers  
**Format**: Plain Text

- **Variant A**: `quick question for {{company}}'s beauty curation`
- **Variant B**: `clean skincare line for {{company}}`
- **Variant C**: `{{first_name}} // indie beauty partnership`

