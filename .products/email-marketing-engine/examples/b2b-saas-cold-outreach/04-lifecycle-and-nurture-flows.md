# 04. Automated Lifecycle & Inbound Nurture Flows

**Brand**: PipePilot  
**Platform**: Loops.so / HubSpot CRM  
**Target Audience**: Inbound demo requests, trial signups, and marketing leads

---

## Flow 1: Inbound Lead & Trial Welcome Flow (5 Emails)

### Email 1: Instant Account Provisioning & Quick Setup (Immediate)
- **Subject**: `[Access Inside] Welcome to PipePilot, {{first_name}}`
- **Preview Text**: `here is your dashboard login plus a 3-minute starter guide.`
- **Content**: Direct login link, API key setup instructions, invitation to join live onboarding workshop.

### Email 2: The Data Decay Reality Check (Day 1)
- **Subject**: `why 30% of your sales database is quietly decaying`
- **Preview Text**: `and the 1 change we made to keep rep contact lists fresh.`
- **Content**: Educational insight on B2B data decay rates and how automated trigger alerts eliminate wasted rep time.

### Email 3: The 5-Minute Pipeline Win (Day 3)
- **Subject**: `how to set up your first hiring intent filter in 4 clicks`
- **Preview Text**: `step-by-step walkthrough to get your first 10 verified prospects.`
- **Content**: Short GIF/image walkthrough showing how to map open roles into immediate enriched buyer contacts.

### Email 4: Objection Buster: "We already have ZoomInfo" (Day 5)
- **Subject**: `"why do we need this if we already pay for ZoomInfo?"`
- **Preview Text**: `how modern outbound teams pair static databases with live trigger agents.`
- **Content**: Case study demonstrating co-existence and synergy with incumbent tools.

### Email 5: The Strategy Call Invitation (Day 7)
- **Subject**: `ready to scale your outbound coverage, {{first_name}}?`
- **Preview Text**: `let our RevOps architects audit your pipeline workflow.`
- **Content**: Direct calendar invitation to a 15-minute pipeline architecture review.

## Flow 2: Free Trial Dropout / Cart Recovery Flow (3 Emails)

### Email 1: Setup Assistance (24 Hours After Trial Abandonment)
- **Subject**: `ran into any questions setting up your integration?`
- **Preview Text**: `our engineering team is here if you need a hand connecting Salesforce.`
- **Content**: Friendly troubleshooting offer, 1-click support reply.

### Email 2: Team Case Study (48 Hours After Abandonment)
- **Subject**: `how AlphaScale booked 24 meetings in their first 14 days`
- **Preview Text**: `the exact 3 filters they activated inside PipePilot.`
- **Content**: Customer proof, ROI breakdown, and direct link back to saved trial setup.

### Email 3: Trial Expiration Notice (72 Hours Before Expiration)
- **Subject**: `your PipePilot trial data expires in 3 days`
- **Preview Text**: `keep your enriched lead lists and custom filters active.`
- **Content**: Clear notification of data retention cutoff and simple 1-click plan selection.
