## 3. Compliance Frameworks

- **Google & Yahoo February 2024 Sender Guidelines**: Fully compliant (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, spam rate < 0.1%, one-click unsubscribe).
- **CAN-SPAM Act (United States)**: Enforces physical postal address, non-deceptive subject lines, and opt-out processing.
- **GDPR (European Union)**: Enforces legitimate interest documentation for B2B cold outreach and explicit double opt-in for B2C lists.
- **CASL (Canada)**: Compatible with Canadian anti-spam regulations and consent tracking.
