## 2. Product Launch 5-Day Broadcast Sequence

### Day 1: Curiosity & Problem Teaser
- **Subject**: `something new is coming this Thursday`
- **Preheader**: `and why the current way of doing {{TOPIC}} is broken.`
- **Content**: Backstory, why existing tools fail, announcement of launch date.

### Day 2: Official Launch & Core Reveal
- **Subject**: `now live: introducing {{PRODUCT_NAME}}`
- **Preheader**: `the complete system for {{DESIRED_OUTCOME}}. Early access inside.`
- **Content**: Full breakdown of deliverables, founding bonus, direct purchase link.

### Day 3: Case Study & Proof Stack
- **Subject**: `how {{CUSTOMER_NAME}} used this to achieve {{METRIC}}`
- **Preheader**: `behind the scenes breakdown of their exact implementation.`
- **Content**: Step-by-step screenshots, tangible outcomes, FAQ section.

### Day 4: 24-Hour Urgency Notice
- **Subject**: `24 hours left for founding bonuses on {{PRODUCT_NAME}}`
- **Preheader**: `the introductory pricing and bonus bundle end tomorrow.`
- **Content**: Reminder of price jump, recap of core bonuses, risk-free guarantee.

### Day 5: Final Call (Sent at 8:00 AM & 8:00 PM)
- **Subject**: `[Closing Tonight] Last chance for {{PRODUCT_NAME}} bonuses`
- **Preheader**: `doors close at 11:59 PM. Final link inside.`
- **Content**: Direct, no-fluff recap, bullet points of everything included, final CTA.
