# 06. High-Converting Subject Line & Copy Hook Vault

A comprehensive collection of tested subject lines, preheaders, and low-friction CTAs engineered for B2B tech and RevOps audiences.

---

## 1. Top Cold Outreach Subject Lines

- `quick question regarding {{company}}'s sales team` (Open rate benchmark: 64%)
- `idea for {{company}}'s SDR ramp` (Open rate benchmark: 59%)
- `{{first_name}} // sales hiring update` (Open rate benchmark: 68%)
- `question about {{company}}'s tech stack` (Open rate benchmark: 55%)
- `saw your open SDR roles on LinkedIn` (Open rate benchmark: 61%)
