# 03. Multi-Touch Cold Email Outbound Sequences

**Campaign**: PipePilot B2B Outbound  
**Audience**: VP of Sales / Head of RevOps  
**Delivery Format**: Plain text (Zero images, 0 links in Step 1)

---

## Step 1: The Context & Trigger Hook (Day 1)

### Subject Line Variants:
- **Variant A**: `quick question regarding {{company}}'s sales team`
- **Variant B**: `idea for {{company}}'s SDR ramp`
- **Variant C**: `{{first_name}} // sales hiring update`

### Body Copy:
```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

Saw that {{company|your team}} is currently scaling the outbound team with new sales roles on LinkedIn.

Most sales leaders tell us the hardest part of adding headcount is the 90-day ramp time while new reps spend half their week manually hunting for contact details.

We built an autonomous enrichment agent that helped [Peer FinTech SaaS] automate 80% of rep prospect research, cutting new hire ramp time from 12 weeks to 22 days.

Worth a 2-minute glance at the workflow map?

Best,
Marcus Vance
PipePilot
```
