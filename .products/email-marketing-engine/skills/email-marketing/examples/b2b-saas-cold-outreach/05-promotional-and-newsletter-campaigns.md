### The Lesson: Timing Beats Volume Every Time

In outbound sales, the team that reaches the buyer within 48 hours of a buying trigger wins 74% of the deals.

Here are the 3 non-negotiables modern sales teams use to win on timing:

1. **Track Trigger Events, Not Job Titles**: Look for new job posts, software migrations, and funding announcements.
2. **Automate the First 80% of Research**: Don't let $70k/year reps do manual copy-paste work.
3. **Keep First Emails Under 75 Words**: Short, conversational notes get read; long essays get deleted.
