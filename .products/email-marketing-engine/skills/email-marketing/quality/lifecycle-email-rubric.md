# Lifecycle & Retention Email Quality Rubric

Score inbound, newsletter, and automated lifecycle emails on a 100-point scale. Campaigns must score **85 points or higher** before staging or activation.

---

## Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)

### 1. Subject Line + Preheader Synergy (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: Subject line generates high curiosity or clear utility (35–55 chars). Preheader extends the thought and creates an irresistible open loop (50–85 chars).
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Good subject line, but preheader is missing or just repeats the subject line.
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: Boring, spammy, or previews raw template code (`View this email in your browser`).

### 2. Information Architecture & Hierarchy (25 Points)
- **22–25 pts (Elite)**: Strict inverted pyramid structure: Gripping headline → Hero visual or core problem → Benefit copy → Single primary CTA. Visual flow naturally guides reader eyes downward.
- **15–21 pts (Acceptable)**: Clear readable layout, but secondary offers distract slightly from the core goal.
- **0–14 pts (Fail)**: Cluttered newsletter with 6 competing banners, no clear focus, disorganized content blocks.

### 3. Copywriting & Value Density (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: High-EEAT, conversational, 100% human voice. Story-driven or insight-dense. Reader gains actionable value even if they choose not to click or purchase.
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Informative and polite, but slightly corporate or generic.
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: Aggressive hard-selling, pure promotion with zero inherent value, AI-generated corporate boilerplate.

### 4. Primary CTA & Action Clarity (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: Exactly ONE primary action. Bulletproof button (HTML/CSS table button, not an image). High-contrast button color. Compelling button copy (benefit-focused, e.g., *"Get Your Free Audit Blueprint →"* instead of *"Submit"* or *"Click Here"*).
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Clear button, but generic label or mild visual competition.
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: No clear button, links buried in walls of text, or 4 conflicting calls to action.

### 5. Technical Deliverability & Mobile Accessibility (15 Points)
- **14–15 pts (Elite)**: Fully responsive (320px–600px). Dark mode tested (logos have transparent backgrounds or light borders). All images have descriptive `alt` tags. Body copy ≥16px. Valid unsubscribe and address in footer.
- **9–13 pts (Acceptable)**: Mobile responsive, but minor dark mode styling glitches or missing 1 alt tag.
- **0–8 pts (Fail)**: Breaks on mobile screens, horizontal scrolling required, image-only email with no live text, broken unsubscribe link.

---

## Passing Benchmark
- **90–100**: Exceptional. Ready for live automated sequencing or broadcast.
- **85–89**: Minor fixes recommended.
- **Below 85**: Block release. Refactor hierarchy and copy.
