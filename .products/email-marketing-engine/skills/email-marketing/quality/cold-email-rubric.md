# Cold Email Quality & Performance Rubric

Score cold email copy on a 100-point scale. Campaigns must score **85 points or higher** before launching.

---

## Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)

### 1. Subject Line & Curiosity Gap (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: 2–5 words, mostly lowercase, sounds like an internal note from a peer (e.g. `quick question about {{company}}`, `idea for {{pain_point}}`, `hiring update`). Zero hype, zero emojis, zero spam triggers.
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Clear, relevant, but slightly sales-leaning or slightly too long (>7 words).
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: All-caps, cheesy questions (`Want more revenue?`), misleading clickbait, or contains spam words.

### 2. Relevance & The Hook / First Sentence (25 Points)
- **22–25 pts (Elite)**: Hyper-relevant first sentence based on an observable trigger event (recent launch, job hiring post, tech stack, podcast mention, funding). Prospect immediately knows this was written for them, not blasted to 10,000 people.
- **15–21 pts (Acceptable)**: Generic personalization (just `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}`), but addresses a genuine pain point for their job title.
- **0–14 pts (Fail)**: "I hope this email finds you well", "My name is John and I work at XYZ company offering award-winning solutions." Immediate delete.

### 3. Value Proposition & Mechanism (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: Solves one distinct, painful friction point using a clear mechanism or specific case study metric (e.g., *"helped [competitor/peer] reduce onboarding churn by 23% in 30 days"*). Concrete, plausible, no buzzwords.
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Good offer, but slightly vague or lacks a specific peer proof point.
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: Laundry list of 8 features, corporate brochure jargon, ungrounded claims ("we are the #1 leader in AI marketing").

### 4. Call-to-Action (CTA) Friction (20 Points)
- **18–20 pts (Elite)**: Interest-based, micro-commitment CTA asking for permission rather than 30 minutes of time (e.g., *"Worth a 3-minute glance?", "Opposed to me sharing a 90-second video walkthrough?", "Open to taking a look at the blueprint?"*).
- **12–17 pts (Acceptable)**: Simple ask, but asks for time (`"Do you have 10 minutes next Tuesday?"`).
- **0–11 pts (Fail)**: Aggressive, high-friction ask: *"Book a 30-minute demo on my Calendly: [link]"* or multiple confusing CTAs.

### 5. Formatting, Brevity & Deliverability (15 Points)
- **14–15 pts (Elite)**: Under 75 words total (4-sentence structure). Clean 1–2 sentence paragraphs. Maximum 1 link or 0 links. Easy to skim in 5 seconds on a mobile phone.
- **9–13 pts (Acceptable)**: 80–120 words, easily readable, but slightly wordy.
- **0–8 pts (Fail)**: Heavy walls of text (>150 words), multiple links, attachments, or heavy HTML templates in cold outreach.

---

## Passing Benchmark
- **90–100**: Ready for live cold outreach. High reply rate expected (8%–15%+).
- **85–89**: Minor polish needed (shorten sentences, soften CTA).
- **Below 85**: Rewrite required. Do not send.
