# Workflow 04: Promotional Broadcasts & Editorial Newsletters

How to write engaging weekly editorial newsletters, design multi-day promotional product launches, and run high-converting flash sales without burning list engagement.

---

## 1. Editorial Newsletter Formats (High Open & Read Rates)

A world-class newsletter should never feel like a corporate brochure. It should read like an insightful dispatch from a respected industry expert.

### Format A: Story → Lesson → Offer (SLO Framework)
1. **The Personal / Real-World Story**: A relatable anecdote, real client problem, or recent observation.
2. **The Epiphany / Core Lesson**: The universal takeaway, contrarian insight, or framework discovered.
3. **The Natural Bridge & Offer**: "If you want help applying this to your system, here's how we can partner..."

### Format B: The 3-2-1 Curated High-Utility Dispatch
- **3 Actionable Frameworks or AI Workflows**
- **2 Quotes or Contrarian Industry Insights**
- **1 Question for the Reader to Ponder / Reply to**

### Format C: The Deep Dive Teardown
- Step-by-step breakdown of an architecture, campaign, or business strategy with diagrams, exact metrics, and implementation steps.

## 2. The 5-Day Product Launch Campaign Engine

When rolling out a new product, service tier, or digital bundle, execute this proven 5-stage broadcast sequence:

```
Day 1: Pre-Launch Curiosity & The "Coming Soon" Teaser
       (Build anticipation, explain why the market needs this, open VIP waitlist).
Day 2: Official Launch Announcement & Core Mechanism
       (The doors are open. Detail the transformation, bonuses, and initial discount).
Day 3: The Case Study / Proof Stack & FAQ
       (Show real customer results, break down objections, answer top 5 questions).
Day 4: The 24-Hour Urgency Notice
       (Clear reminder that the founding discount or bonus package closes tomorrow).
Day 5: The "Closing Tonight at Midnight" Last Call
       (Send 2 emails: Morning reminder + Final 4-hour countdown warning).
```

## 3. High-CTR Subject Line & Preview Text Architecture

The subject line sells the open; the preview text sells the first sentence.

### Formula Types:
- **Curiosity Gap**: `the $42k mistake we almost shipped` (Preview: `and the 1 line of code that prevented it.`)
- **Actionable Utility / How-to**: `how to audit your cold email deliverability in 5 mins` (Preview: `step-by-step SPF, DKIM, and DMARC walkthrough.`)
- **Contrarian Truth**: `stop sending 30-minute meeting links` (Preview: `why micro-commitments convert 4x higher.`)
- **Personal / Lowercase**: `quick question regarding your stack` (Preview: `noticed something interesting on your career page.`)
- **Direct Benefit**: `new template: the 4-sentence outbound sequence` (Preview: `copy, paste, and deploy directly to Instantly or Apollo.`)

---

## 4. Unsubscribe & List Hygiene Etiquette

- Always provide a clear, visible 1-click unsubscribe option.
- Allow subscribers to "opt out of this launch" while remaining on the weekly editorial newsletter.
- Clean and prune inactive subscribers (>120 days unopened) every quarter to maintain high inbox deliverability.
