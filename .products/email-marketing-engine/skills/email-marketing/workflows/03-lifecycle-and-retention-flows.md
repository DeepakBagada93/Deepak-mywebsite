# Workflow 03: Lifecycle, Onboarding & Retention Flows

How to engineer automated, trigger-based email flows that nurture inbound leads, onboard new customers, recover lost checkouts, and maximize customer lifetime value (LTV).

---

## 1. The Core 5 Lifecycle Flows

Every business (whether B2B SaaS, E-Commerce, Agency, or Digital Creator) requires these five automated revenue engines:

```
[Visitor / Prospect]
       │
       ├──► 1. Welcome / Onboarding Sequence (New Subscriber / Sign-up)
       ├──► 2. Lead Magnet Delivery & Bridge Flow (Content Opt-in)
       ├──► 3. Abandoned Checkout / Cart Recovery Flow (High-Intent Dropout)
       ├──► 4. Post-Purchase Delight & VIP Referral Flow (Customer Retention)
       └──► 5. Win-Back & Re-Engagement Flow (Dormant Customer Revival)
```

---

## 2. Flow 1: Welcome & Onboarding Sequence (5 Emails)

- **Goal**: Establish immediate relationship, set expectations, deliver quick win, introduce core mechanism.
- **Timing**:
  - **Email 1 (Day 0 — Immediate)**: Delivery of promised asset + Origin Story hook + Whitelisting request.
  - **Email 2 (Day 1)**: The "Epiphany Bridge" (Why traditional approaches fail and the new paradigm).
  - **Email 3 (Day 3)**: Actionable Quick Win / Case Study walkthrough (High-utility value).
  - **Email 4 (Day 5)**: Overcoming the #1 Objection / Addressing hidden fears.
  - **Email 5 (Day 7)**: The Soft Offer / Community Invitation / Next Step CTA.

---

## 3. Flow 2: Abandoned Checkout / Cart Recovery (3 Emails)

- **Timing & Strategy**:
  - **Email 1 (1 Hour Post-Abandonment — The Helpful Assistant)**:
    - Assume technical glitch or distraction.
    - Plain, friendly tone. "Did life get in the way?"
    - Direct link back to saved cart with dynamic product thumbnail. Zero discount offered here.
  - **Email 2 (20 Hours Post-Abandonment — Social Proof & FAQ)**:
    - Address common friction points (shipping times, guarantees, sizing, integrations).
    - Insert 2–3 customer reviews / testimonials.
  - **Email 3 (48 Hours Post-Abandonment — Urgency & Incentive)**:
    - Cart reservation expiration warning.
    - Optional small incentive (e.g., free priority shipping, 10% code, or free bonus template).
    - Clear expiration deadline (e.g., 24 hours).

---

## 4. Flow 3: Post-Purchase Delight & Retention (4 Emails)

- **Goal**: Prevent buyer remorse, ensure product adoption, generate 5-star reviews, drive repeat purchases.
- **Timing & Strategy**:
  - **Email 1 (Immediate)**: Order confirmation + Celebration + What happens next roadmap.
  - **Email 2 (Day 3 or upon shipment/access)**: "How to get the most out of [Product]" (Video or quick guide).
  - **Email 3 (Day 10)**: "How did everything go?" (Review request or satisfaction check).
  - **Email 4 (Day 21)**: Personalized cross-sell or VIP referral invitation.

---

## 5. Flow 4: Customer Win-Back (3 Emails)

- **Trigger**: 60 to 90 days without purchase or app activity.
- **Email 1**: "We've made some major updates since you were last here" (Feature/collection roundup).
- **Email 2**: "Here's a gift to welcome you back" (Exclusive comeback credit).
- **Email 3**: "Is it time to say goodbye?" (1-click unsubscribe or preference update).
