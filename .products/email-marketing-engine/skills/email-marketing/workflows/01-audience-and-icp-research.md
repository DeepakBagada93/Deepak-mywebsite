# Workflow 01: Audience, ICP & Competitor Email Research

How to conduct evidence-based audience research, define B2B Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and B2C buyer personas, identify actionable buying triggers, and perform competitor email teardowns.

---

## Step 1: Define the ICP (B2B) or Buyer Persona (B2C)

### For B2B Cold Outreach & Account-Based Sequences:
1. **Firmographic Criteria**:
   - Company Size (Headcount, e.g., 20–100 employees, 100–500, or Enterprise 1000+).
   - Annual Revenue / Funding Stage (Bootstrapped $2M-$10M ARR, Seed/Series A, Pre-IPO).
   - Industry & Vertical (e.g., B2B FinTech SaaS, Direct-to-Consumer Apparel, Industrial Automation).
   - Geography / Timezone (North America, UK/EMEA, APAC).
2. **Decision-Maker Persona (Buyer vs Champion vs User)**:
   - **Job Titles**: Head of Growth, VP of Marketing, CTO, Founder & CEO, Director of RevOps.
   - **KPIs & Core Responsibilities**: What metric is their bonus tied to? (e.g. CAC, ARR, sprint velocity, pipeline conversion).
   - **Daily Headaches & Latent Friction**: What manual, broken, or frustrating task consumes 5 hours of their week?
   - **Language & Jargon**: The exact terminology they use on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and internal Slack channels (e.g. "PLG churn", "LTV:CAC ratio", "reconciliation errors").

### For B2C E-Commerce & Subscription Lifecycle:
- Core Motivations, Lifestyle Aspirations, Anxieties, Objections to Buying, Average Cart Value expectations.

---

## Step 2: Uncover Actionable Buying Triggers

Cold emails with trigger events convert 3x–5x higher than generic cold emails. Identify which triggers indicate immediate buying intent:

1. **Hiring / Team Expansion**:
   - Company is hiring 3+ Account Executives or Engineers → Need automation, onboarding tooling, or pipeline support.
2. **Recent Funding / Capital Raise**:
   - Announced Seed or Series A on Crunchbase → Mandate to scale acquisition within 90 days.
3. **Tech Stack Changes**:
   - Added HubSpot, migrated to Shopify Plus, or installed Segment (detectable via BuiltWith / Wappalyzer).
4. **Leadership Transition**:
   - New VP of Marketing hired 45 days ago → Eager to test new playbooks and audit previous vendor contracts.
5. **Product / Feature Launch**:
   - Released new version on Product Hunt or press release → High visibility, team is active and responsive.

---

## Step 3: Competitor Email Teardowns & Swipe File Analysis

1. **Newsletter / Lifecycle Subscription**:
   - Sign up for 3–5 direct competitors' newsletters using burner or testing accounts.
   - Trigger their abandoned cart or signup flow.
   - Document:
     - Sending frequency and delivery time.
     - Subject line styles (curiosity vs discount vs educational).
     - Visual design (HTML-heavy vs minimal text).
     - Offer discounts (e.g., 10% vs 20% vs free shipping vs bundle).
2. **Identify Market Fatigue & White Space**:
   - What are all competitors saying that makes them sound identical?
   - Where can our copy take a fresh, contrarian, or hyper-specific stance?
