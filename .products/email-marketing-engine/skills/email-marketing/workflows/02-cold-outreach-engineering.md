# Workflow 02: Cold Outreach & B2B Sequence Engineering

How to engineer high-converting B2B cold email sequences that land in the primary inbox, generate positive replies, and book qualified sales pipeline.

---

## 1. The 4-Sentence Cold Email Architecture

The best cold emails look like personal notes sent from an iPhone between meetings. Aim for **50–85 words maximum**.

```
Sentence 1: The Context / Trigger Hook
            (Shows you know who they are and why you're reaching out today).
Sentence 2: The Pain / Mechanism Observation
            (Pinpoints the specific operational bottleneck they likely face).
Sentence 3: The Social Proof / Tangible Result
            (1 specific metric achieved for an identical peer in their space).
Sentence 4: The Low-Friction Soft CTA
            (Asks for permission or interest, never for their calendar).
```

### Example:
> **Subject**: quick idea for {{company}}
>
> Hi {{first_name}}, noticed you recently posted 3 new SDR roles on LinkedIn.
>
> Most outbound teams find ramp-time takes 90 days because reps spend half their week manually researching prospect tech stacks.
>
> We built a lightweight enrichment workflow that automated 80% of that research for [Competitor/Peer], cutting ramp time to 3 weeks and doubling booked demos.
>
> Worth a 2-minute glance at the workflow diagram?

## 2. Multi-Touch Cold Sequence Blueprint (4–6 Steps)

A complete sequence balances persistence with respect, rotating value angles rather than repeatedly asking "did you see my email?".

### Step 1: The Trigger Hook (Day 1)
- Contextual opening tied to trigger event.
- Core mechanism and micro-case study.
- Low-friction interest CTA.
- Plain text, 0 links, 0 images.

### Step 2: The Video Teardown / Asset Drop (Day 4 — Threaded Reply)
- Reply to Step 1 in the same email thread.
- *"Hey {{first_name}}, put together a quick 90-second Loom showing exactly how we mapped this out for {{company}}'s checkout. Mind if I drop the link over?"*
- (Asking permission before dropping video link protects spam score and boosts curiosity).

### Step 3: The Quantified Peer Proof Case Study (Day 8 — Threaded Reply)
- Deep dive into one tangible transformation.
- Highlight the "Before", the "Breakthrough", and the "After" metric.
- *"Thought you might find this interesting — when [Similar Brand] had the same bottleneck, here's what shifted..."*

### Step 4: The Contrarian Angle / Reverse Logic (Day 13 — Threaded Reply)
- Challenge a common industry belief.
- e.g., *"Most founders think hiring 5 more SDRs fixes pipeline, but usually it just amplifies bad data..."*

### Step 5: The Priority Shift / Polite Breakup (Day 18 — Threaded Reply)
- Gracefully acknowledge that this might not be a top priority right now.
- *"Hey {{first_name}}, guessing your hands are full with the Q4 sprint right now. I'll take a step back and won't keep following up. If timing aligns later, feel free to reach back out anytime."*
- (Often yields the highest response rate because pressure is completely removed).

## 4. Cold Email Copywriting Rules

1. **Subject Lines**: 2–5 words, lowercase or sentence case. No sales words (`"boost revenue"`, `"free trial"`, `"partnership"`).
2. **First Line Rule**: The first 12 words show in the prospect's mobile preview. Ensure those 12 words are about THEM, not about you or your company.
3. **Never Pitch Features**: Sell the vacation, not the plane engine. Sell the result (23% pipeline lift), not the database integration.
4. **Soft CTA Primacy**:
   - ❌ *"Can we hop on a 30-min call this Thursday at 2pm?"*
   - ✅ *"Worth a quick 3-minute look?"*
   - ✅ *"Opposed to me sending a 1-page breakdown?"*
   - ✅ *"Should I speak with someone else on your team?"*
