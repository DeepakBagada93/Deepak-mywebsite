## 1. Market Opportunity & Problem Validation

### The Core Problem
Email marketing remains the highest-ROI channel in digital commerce ($36–$42 returned per $1 spent). However, recent structural shifts have disrupted legacy workflows:
1. **Google & Yahoo February 2024 Sender crackdowns**: Tens of thousands of businesses had their primary domains blacklisted or penalized due to unauthenticated DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and spam rates exceeding 0.10%.
2. **AI Fluff & Email Saturation**: Generic LLM prompts produce identifiable, buzzword-heavy essays ("I hope this email finds you well", "In today's fast-paced digital landscape") that prospects instantly ignore.
3. **High Agency / Tooling Costs**: Retainers for email agencies average $2,500–$6,000/month, while full-time SDR salaries exceed $75,000 + commission.
