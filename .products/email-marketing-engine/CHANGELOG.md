## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Production Skill Construction**: Core `email-marketing` skill with 6-phase engineering pipeline.
- **B2B Cold Outreach Engine**: 4-sentence cold architecture (<85 words), multi-touch 5-step threaded follow-up sequences, and interest-based soft CTAs.
- **Automated Lifecycle & Retention Flows**: 5-part Welcome/Onboarding, 3-part Abandoned Cart/Checkout recovery, Post-Purchase delight, and Customer Win-Back flows.
- **Editorial Newsletters & Launches**: Weekly Story-Lesson-Offer (SLO) editorial dispatches and 5-day promotional product launch sequences.
- **Technical Deliverability Standards**: Full blueprints for Google & Yahoo February 2024 compliance, SPF, 2048-bit DKIM, DMARC policy rollout, and mailbox warmup rotation schedules.
- **Algorithmic Spam Word Scanner**: 300+ categorized trigger words with high-converting, inbox-safe replacements.
- **Automated Verification CLI**: Zero-dependency `validate-email-campaign.mjs` script verifying file completeness, spam score, token syntax, and sequence depth.
- **Reference Examples**: Complete working B2B SaaS cold outreach campaign and D2C E-commerce retention campaign.
- **Comprehensive Documentation**: Complete `product-specification.md`, `tutorial-guide.md`, `store-listing.md`, `test-plan.md`, and `test-results.md`.
