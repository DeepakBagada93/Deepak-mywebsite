# Market Research Dossier: Deep Research & Competitive Dossier Engine

## 1. Market Opportunity
With the rise of Perplexity Pro, OpenAI Deep Research, and Grok 3 DeepSearch, clients and consultants expect multi-page, evidence-backed research dossiers rather than 1-paragraph LLM summaries.
However:
- Perplexity / ChatGPT Enterprise accounts cost $200–$500/year per seat and do not integrate directly into custom agency pipelines.
- Strategy consultants and agency strategists routinely spend 12–20 hours writing competitive intelligence reports for new client discovery calls.
- This self-hosted n8n engine produces 10-page formatted markdown and PDF reports via a single webhook trigger for less than $0.15 in API costs per run.
