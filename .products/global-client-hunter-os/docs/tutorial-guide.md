# Customer Tutorial & Quickstart Guide: Global Client Hunter OS

Welcome to **Global Client Hunter OS**! This guide will get you from zero to your first high-converting international pitch and $3k–$5k proposal in under 3 minutes.

---

## ⚡ 3-Minute Quickstart

### Step 1: Install the Skill
- **Google Antigravity**: Place the `global-client-hunter-os` directory inside your workspace's `skills/` folder.
- **Claude Code**: Add `skills/global-client-hunter-os/SKILL.md` to your system rules or prompt directory.
- **Cursor / Codex**: Reference `SKILL.md` in your `.cursorrules` or paste into the conversation.

### Step 2: Pick Your Target Prospect
Find an international tech company (US, UK, EU, or Gulf) with an active product, a job listing, or a public presence.

### Step 3: Run the Acquisition Prompt
Copy and paste this prompt into your agent:

```markdown
Run Global Client Hunter OS:
- Target Company: https://pulseflow-app.com
- Decision Maker: Sarah Miller, VP of Product & Engineering
- Observation: Job board has had an open "Senior React & Node Engineer" role for 45 days. Their app dashboard has sluggish table rendering.
- My Specialty: Next.js 15, TanStack Table optimization, React Server Components.
- Proof of Work: Optimized high-volume data grids for a FinTech app, improving frame rate from 18fps to 60fps.
- Target Deal: $3,500 Performance Sprint (2 weeks).
```

### Step 4: Review and Send
The agent will instantly generate:
1. Three distinct outreach angles (Teardown, Hiring Relief, Case Study).
2. Follow-up sequences for Day 4 and Day 8.
3. An executive-ready proposal ready to send the moment they reply.

## 💡 Real-World Outreach Recipes

### Recipe 1: The "Unfilled Job Post" Angle
When a US tech firm has had an engineering role open for over a month, they are losing velocity.
* **Why it works**: You offer immediate capacity without the 6-week recruitment overhead and recruiter commission.
* **Agent command**: `Generate a Hiring Relief Pitch for [Company URL] targeting [Open Role].`

### Recipe 2: The "Frontend Speed Teardown"
Run PageSpeed Insights or inspect network requests on a prospect's public app.
* **Why it works**: You prove competence before asking for a dollar.
* **Agent command**: `Generate a Teardown Outreach highlighting [Specific Web Vitals Issue] with a 3-step solution.`

## 🏦 Cross-Border Payments: The Indian Agency Checklist

1. **Invoicing**: Use Stripe Invoicing, Wise Business, or Razorpay International.
2. **0% GST (LUT)**: File Form GST RFD-11 (Letter of Undertaking) on the GST portal to export services at 0% IGST without tax blockage.
3. **FIRC / e-BRC**: Ensure your bank issues a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) for every inward remittance.
