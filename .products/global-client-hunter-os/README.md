## ⚡ What is Global Client Hunter OS?

The **Global Client Hunter OS** is a battle-tested AI agent skill and operational framework engineered to help Indian software engineers, AI developers, and boutique agencies systematically win high-paying overseas clients.

It replaces generic "Dear Sir / Madam" pitches with:
1. **Automated ICP Friction Auditing**: Dissects a target company's stack, jobs, and operational bottlenecks.
2. **0% Synthetic Cold Outreach**: Crafts razor-sharp, 90-word emails and LinkedIn notes that get read by CTOs and Founders.
3. **Executive Scope & Proposal Generation**: Generates 3-tier milestone proposals structured around business ROI.
4. **Cross-Border Finance & Export Compliance**: Guides foreign currency pricing, Wise/Stripe setups, and 0% GST LUT export compliance.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Under 3 Minutes)

1. **Install into Antigravity or Claude Code**:
   Copy the `skills/global-client-hunter-os/` folder into your agent's skills directory.
2. **Trigger the Skill**:
   Feed the agent a target company URL and your technical specialty:
   ```markdown
   Run Global Client Hunter OS:
   - Target Company: https://example-fintech.io
   - Target Persona: VP of Engineering
   - My Stack: Next.js 15, FastAPI, AI Agents, PostgreSQL
   - Target Sprint: $4,500 Full-Stack Modernization
   ```
3. **Execute & Send**:
   Review the generated 3-touch sequence, copy into your email client or LinkedIn, and track responses.
