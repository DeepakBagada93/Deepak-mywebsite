# Scripts, shot lists and creator briefs — Nara Vitamin C Face Serum

Style: **UGC testimonial · front-camera** · Runtime: **30s** · Angles: **6**

## The brief this pack was built from

- Buyer: women aged 24-35 in Indian metros who are tired of dull, uneven-looking skin
- Pain: dull, tired-looking skin that makes me look exhausted even after eight hours of sleep
- Promise: a brighter-looking, more even finish in your existing morning routine
- Proof: Sold 4,200 bottles in the last six months, with a 4.6 average from 870 verified reviews.
- Offer: the 30ml bottle with a seven-day routine card at ₹799
- Objections: Vitamin C serums always oxidise before I finish them / It will pill under my sunscreen

## Cost of inaction (`pain-agitate`)

Hook (10 words, 3 hook signals): **I wasted two years on dull, tired-looking skin before this.**

Why it works: Names the daily cost of the problem before the product appears, so the product feels like relief.

| # | Time | Beat | Spoken | On-screen text | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–3s | Hook | I wasted two years on dull, tired-looking skin before this. | dull, tired-looking skin | creator leans into the front camera mid-sentence, product still out of frame |
| 2 | 3–11s | Agitate | Every morning it was the same: concealer first, coffee second, and still looking tired in photos. I tried ten-step routines and random viral serums and nothing stuck. | same routine, same result | creator glances at a makeup brush on the counter, then back to the camera, small frustrated exhale |
| 3 | 11–19s | Mechanism | Then I switched to Nara Vitamin C Face Serum. 10% stabilised vitamin C with ferulic acid, in a lightweight base that absorbs before sunscreen. | the switch | product enters frame label-first, one clean demonstration, no jump cuts |
| 4 | 19–27s | Payoff | Here is the part I checked before buying: Sold 4,200 bottles in the last six months, with a 4.6 average from 870 verified reviews. | the difference | creator looks at the product with a small genuine reaction, no exaggerated smile |
| 5 | 27–30s | CTA | Tap the link and check the full routine at ₹799. | link below | product held beside the face, bottom third clear for the caption and CTA |

Creator brief:

- Cast: the same creator in every shot: Maya, a 29-year-old woman, slim build, warm medium skin, round face, faint freckles, expressive dark eyes, dark shoulder-length hair with a centre part, wearing an oversized cream cotton shirt and small gold hoops, with the product held at chest height.
- Wardrobe continuity: keep the same outfit, hair and jewellery in every scene.
- Product handling: label square to camera in the first and last scene; no extra props on the label.
- Performance: talk to one person, not to a crowd. Keep energy below 6/10. No pointing at imaginary text.
- Do not say: guaranteed, cure, instant, 100%, or any result the product page cannot prove.
