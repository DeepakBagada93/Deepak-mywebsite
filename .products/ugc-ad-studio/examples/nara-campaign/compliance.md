# Compliance and claims — Nara Vitamin C Face Serum

This is a production checklist, not legal advice. A human signs off before publishing.

## Disclosure

- Required for this style: **yes**
- Caption disclosure: Ad · AI-generated creator, real product. Results vary.
- Label realistic AI-generated presenters as AI-generated on the platform, not only in the caption.
- Turn on the paid-partnership / branded-content toggle when money or product changed hands.

## Rules enforced by this pack

- Use the exact character, product and grade blocks in every prompt; drift is the main cause of unusable ads.
- Disclose AI-generated presenters and paid partnerships on every platform that requires it.
- Do not imply results that the buyer cannot substantiate on the landing page.
- Keep before-and-after frames under identical lighting, framing and grade; do not retouch the result.
- Use licensed music only; platform libraries are the safe default.
- Never invent reviews, testimonials, user counts, certifications or medical claims.

## Claims rewritten during the build

- None. The brief did not contain blocked claim patterns.

## Before you publish

- [ ] Every claim on the landing page matches a claim in the ad.
- [ ] The price in the ad matches the checkout.
- [ ] Someone with the product has watched the final cut.
- [ ] Music is licensed for commercial use.
- [ ] The AI-generated and paid-partnership disclosures are visible.
