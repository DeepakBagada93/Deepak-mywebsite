## 1. Executive Summary & Problem

A recurring tragedy in developer-led startups is **the 0.5% conversion wall**:
1. **Technical Jargon Overload**: Developers headline their landing page with implementation details ("A high-throughput distributed graph neural-net proxy") instead of business outcomes ("Save 14 hours per week debugging database deadlocks").
2. **Missing Objection Handling**: Visitors leave without buying because questions about security, pricing cancellation, and tech-stack integration are left unanswered.
3. **Weak Call-to-Actions (CTAs)**: Generic buttons like "Submit" or "Click Here" instead of high-intent outcome CTAs ("Start Free 14-Day Trial", "Harden Your App Now").

The **CRO Landing Page Architect** provides an automated landing page conversion audit tool and a multi-client agent skill that systematically transforms low-converting technical pages into high-converting conversion funnels.
