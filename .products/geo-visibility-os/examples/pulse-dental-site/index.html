What does cosmetic dentistry include?

Cosmetic dentistry covers treatments that change the appearance of your teeth and smile. At Pulse Dental Studio the most requested options are porcelain veneers, single-tooth and full-arch implants, same-day crowns, professional whitening and bonding. Each option solves a different problem. Veneers change the shape and shade of front teeth. Implants replace a missing root and crown. Same-day crowns restore a damaged tooth in one visit when the case allows it. Whitening and bonding are the least invasive starting points for shade and small chips. A consultation should tell you which of these actually fits your case, and which ones are unnecessary.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost in Austin?

There is no single price for cosmetic dentistry because the scope changes per tooth and per material. The main cost drivers are the number of teeth treated, the lab and material chosen, whether sedation is used, and whether a second visit is needed. A fair quote lists each procedure, the lab fee, the sedation fee and what happens if the case needs more work. Ask for that breakdown in writing. In 2025, 92% of our treatment plans included a written estimate before the first appointment, and 78% of those estimates matched the final invoice within 5%. The remaining difference came from cases that needed an additional crown or a lab remake, which we now flag in the plan.

Who is a good candidate?

Good candidates are adults with stable gum health, treated decay and realistic expectations about colour and shape. We do not recommend veneers for someone who grinds heavily at night without a guard, or for a patient who wants a guarantee that the result will never change. Implants need enough bone volume, or a graft plan. Same-day crowns depend on the tooth and the bite. The honest answer is that a short exam decides this, not a website. If your case is outside our scope, we will refer you rather than stretch the treatment.

What should you ask before booking?

Ask who does the work, what happens if a veneer chips, what the lab warranty covers, and whether the quote includes follow-up visits. Ask for the dentist's licence number and the lab's name. Ask how many similar cases the studio completed in the last 12 months. In 2026, 64% of new patients told us they chose a provider because the first consultation explained the trade-offs clearly, not because of the lowest price. That is the standard we hold ourselves to. Our own limit is simple: we can describe the process and the cost drivers, but we cannot promise a specific outcome for your case before an exam.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer sedation for cosmetic work?

Yes. Pulse Dental Studio offers sedation options for appropriate cases. The team reviews your health history and explains the risks before the appointment. Sedation is not right for every patient, and the final decision belongs to the dentist after an exam.

How long do veneers last?

Many porcelain veneers last 10 to 15 years with good care, but the range depends on the bite, habits and maintenance. Chips can be repaired. We document the material and the lab so a future repair matches.
