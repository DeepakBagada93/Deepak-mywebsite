About Pulse Dental Studio

Pulse Dental Studio is a cosmetic dentistry practice in Austin, TX, serving adults 30-55 who want veneers, implants or same-day crowns. We publish our approach, our credentials and our limits because a website should help you judge a provider, not just impress you. This page explains who does the work, how we plan treatment, where we practise, and what we will not claim.

Written by Dr. Maya Patel, DDS · Updated October 12, 2026

Who does the work?

Dr. Maya Patel, DDS, leads the clinical team. She completed her dental training in Texas and has focused on restorative and cosmetic cases since 2014. The practice works with a licensed local lab, and the lab name is written on every treatment plan. Dr. Patel reviews the plan with you before any preparation. If a case needs an oral surgeon, a periodontist or an orthodontist, we refer you and stay involved in the planning. We do not present a specialist's work as our own, and we do not use stock photos of people who are not patients.

How do we plan treatment?

Every new patient starts with an exam, photographs and a conversation about what you want to change. We then write a plan that lists the procedures, the order, the cost drivers and the alternatives. You get a copy. If the plan depends on a lab result or a specialist opinion, we say so before you commit. In 2024 we introduced a written-plan standard, and in 2025 92% of our treatment plans included a written estimate before the first appointment. We track whether the final invoice matches the estimate because surprises are a trust problem, not just a billing problem.

Where do we practise?

The studio is at 2100 South Lamar Boulevard, Suite 120, Austin, TX 78704. We serve Austin, Round Rock and Cedar Park. Availability changes, so confirm the current service area and hours before you book. We do not claim to be the best practice in Austin, and we do not publish invented awards. What we can show is the process, the credentials, the lab relationship and the aftercare policy. If those do not fit what you need, another provider may be the better choice, and we would rather say that early.

What standards do we hold?

We keep the visible content, the structured data and the business listings consistent. We name the limits of each treatment, we ask for consent before using any patient example, and we do not buy reviews or pay for editorial placement. In 2026, 64% of new patients said the first consultation explained the trade-offs clearly, which is the outcome we measure. We also review our pages every quarter so the dates, services and pricing language stay accurate. Our limit is simple: we can explain the process and the range of outcomes, but we cannot promise a specific result before an exam.
