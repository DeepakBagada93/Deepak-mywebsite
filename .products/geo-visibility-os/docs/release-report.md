# Release report — GEO Visibility OS v1.1.0

Status: READY FOR OWNER APPROVAL · Date: 2026-11-01 · Owner: Deepak Bagada Systems

## What is shipping

GEO Visibility OS is a local, zero-dependency GEO/AEO audit and fix system for US SEO agencies, consultants and brands. Version 1.1.0 includes:

- A four-stage skill workflow: capture, build, audit, deliver.
- `scan-site.mjs` for local snapshots and public URLs.
- `build-geo-pack.mjs` for the eleven-file client pack.
- `audit-geo-pack.mjs` with 40 weighted checks across eight classes.
- A 30-prompt buyer panel, six content briefs, five schema blocks, a 25+ action roadmap, a ten-source citation plan, a measurement plan and a white-label report.
- A fictional dental sample, a deliberately broken sample and expected audit reports.
- 27 structural and functional tests, including URL safety, brief/pack validation and a local HTTP scanner fixture.
- Security and production-readiness documentation with a private-target blocklist and fetch caps.

## Market basis

The product was selected from a scored six-opportunity US market review (`docs/research-report.md`). GEO scored 27/30 on demand, willingness to pay, competition, deliverability and evidence. Supporting signals:

- 68.01% US zero-click rate, January–April 2026 (SparkToro/Similarweb).
- AI Overviews associated with ~58% lower position-1 CTR (Ahrefs).
- A field experiment showing AI Overviews cut outbound clicks 39.8% and raised zero-click 34.5% (TSE).
- 51% of B2B buyers starting in an AI chatbot (G2, 2026).
- GEO in 55% of client briefs for adopters vs 8% for non-adopters (Demand-Genius).
- 71% of agencies spend more time explaining GEO than executing it (Scribewise).
- 88% of CMOs asked about AI visibility, 34% have a strategy (Corporate Ink).

These are dated observations, not forecasts of this product's revenue.

## Design decisions

1. **Audit, not rank tracker.** Live AI querying creates cost, rate limits and irreproducible results. A deterministic signal audit plus a manual prompt panel is sellable today and does not require API keys.
2. **Forty checks, eight classes, 100 points.** The weights emphasize crawl access, entity clarity and content extractability, which are the levers a client can actually change.
3. **Three verdicts with hard gates.** Any critical finding forces `DO NOT SHIP`; high findings block `PASS` even above 85. This prevents a high average from hiding a fatal flaw.
4. **No snake oil.** `llms.txt` is explicitly optional. The pack rejects paid reviews, link buying and astroturfing, and requires a no-guarantee statement in every deliverable.
5. **White-label by default.** The report carries the agency name and is written for a client, not for the operator.
6. **Local-first and deterministic.** Local scans need no network. URL scans are optional, fetch only public text/HTML/XML/JSON and block private/local targets by default. No secrets, no telemetry, and the same brief and snapshot always produce the same pack.

## Known limitations

- The scan does not execute JavaScript or authenticate.
- URL scanning is not a hardened multi-tenant fetcher; see `docs/security-and-privacy.md` for residual risk.
- The prompt panel is manual; there is no live engine integration.
- The readiness score is a diagnostic, not a forecast.
- Citation sources are templates with actions, not pre-researched URLs.
- Business claims are not verified.
- The product is US-first in research, pricing and source selection.
- The license and pricing require owner approval.
- No real client engagement or outcome has been attached.
