# Market Research Dossier: Autonomous AI SDR & Inbound Lead Closer

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Demand
Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) represent one of the highest churn, highest cost roles in B2B agencies and SaaS startups. According to Bridge Group SaaS benchmarks:
- Average US SDR base salary: **$55,000 – $75,000 / year**.
- Average ramp time: **3.2 months**.
- Inbound lead response speed: Leads contacted within **5 minutes** have a **21x higher qualification rate** compared to leads contacted after 30 minutes.

Commercial AI SDR platforms (such as 11x.ai, Artisan, and Regie.ai) charge subscription fees starting at **$1,000 – $2,500/month**. This creates massive demand for a **one-time purchase, self-hosted n8n automation system** that founders and agencies can deploy in 15 minutes.
