# 04. Lifecycle & Nurture Flows — Agency Services

Four flows cover the agency revenue path: welcome, proposal checkout dropout, onboarding, and win-back.

---

## Flow 1 — Welcome & Onboarding (5 emails)

**Trigger:** New subscriber from the site, lead magnet, or newsletter.

| # | Timing | Subject | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediate | `the handoff map you asked for` | Deliver the asset, set expectations |
| 2 | Day 2 | `the one handoff nobody owns` | Teach the core idea, one example |
| 3 | Day 4 | `a 41% faster launch, in four screens` | Proof, no hard pitch |
| 4 | Day 7 | `what we would fix first at {{company}}` | Invite a diagnostic reply |
| 5 | Day 10 | `the small entry scope` | Present a low-risk first engagement |

Welcome email body:

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

Here is the handoff map: {{asset_url}}. It takes about four minutes to read.

Most teams find one of the four handoffs is unowned. If you reply with the one that feels familiar, I will send a specific note on how to fix it.

{{sender_name|Alex}}
```

---

## Flow 2 — Proposal Checkout Dropout (3 emails)

**Trigger:** Proposal viewed twice but not signed after 48 hours.

| # | Timing | Subject | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 48h | `one question before you decide` | Surface the real objection |
| 2 | 5 days | `the smaller way to start` | Reduce scope, not price |
| 3 | 10 days | `closing the file` | Clean close, keep the door open |

The “checkout” here is proposal acceptance. Treat the signature page as the conversion event and remove every unnecessary step before it.

---

## Flow 3 — Client Onboarding (4 emails)

| # | Timing | Subject | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately after signature | `your kickoff starts here` | Access, owners, first checkpoint |
| 2 | Day 1 | `the 72-hour plan` | Reduce anxiety, name the first deliverable |
| 3 | Day 7 | `first win, documented` | Show early progress, ask for one correction |
| 4 | Day 21 | `the next 60 days` | Review, expand scope only after a result |

---

## Flow 4 — Client Win-Back (3 emails)

**Trigger:** No project or retainer activity for 60–90 days.

| # | Timing | Subject | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Day 60 | `it has been a while — here is what changed` | Three concrete updates |
| 2 | Day 67 | `the smaller new scope` | Re-entry offer, no guilt |
| 3 | Day 75 | `closing the loop for good` | Suppress if no reply |

Never send win-back to a client with an open invoice or an unresolved delivery issue.
