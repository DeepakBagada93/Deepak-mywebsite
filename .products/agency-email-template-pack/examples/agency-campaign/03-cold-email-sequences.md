# 03. Multi-Touch Cold Email Sequence — Agency Services

**Audience:** Founders and marketing leaders at 15–120 person B2B SaaS companies  
**Format:** Plain text, one idea per touch, no calendar link in Step 1  
**Sequence length:** 5 touches over 18 days

---

## Step 1 — The Trigger Hook (Day 1)

Subject variants:
- `quick question about {{company}}'s launch page`
- `saw the new marketing hire`
- `{{first_name}} // one handoff question`

Body:

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

Saw {{trigger_event|the new marketing hire}} at {{company|your team}}. Most teams in that spot inherit a campaign calendar that is already behind.

The bottleneck is rarely design capacity. It is the handoff between the strategy doc and the person building the page.

We mapped that handoff for {{peer_company|a similar SaaS team}} and cut launch time from six weeks to nine days.

Worth a 2-minute look at the map? No call needed.

{{sender_name|Alex}}
{{brand_name|Northline Studio}}
```

Rules: 4 sentences, under 85 words, zero links, no attachment.

---

## Step 2 — The Teardown (Day 4, threaded reply)

Subject: `Re: quick question about {{company}}'s launch page`

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

I recorded a 90-second walkthrough of the handoff map: brief, page, review, ship. It shows where the three-week delay usually enters.

Want me to send the private link?

{{sender_name|Alex}}
```

---

## Step 3 — Quantified Peer Proof (Day 8, threaded reply)

Subject: `Re: quick question about {{company}}'s launch page`

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

One data point from a recent engagement: {{client_name|a Series A SaaS team}} had four campaigns queued and one designer.

We rebuilt the handoff so the strategist wrote the page brief and the designer shipped from a fixed component set. Result: {{metric_delta|41%}} faster launch time and two fewer revision rounds per page.

Want the one-page teardown?

{{sender_name|Alex}}
```

---

## Step 4 — The Contrarian Angle (Day 13, threaded reply)

Subject: `Re: quick question about {{company}}'s launch page`

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

Most agencies solve a slow launch by adding another designer or a project manager.

In our experience that adds coordination cost without removing the real delay: nobody owns the brief.

Open to seeing how your current handoff compares to the map?

{{sender_name|Alex}}
```

---

## Step 5 — The Polite Close (Day 18, threaded reply)

Subject: `Re: quick question about {{company}}'s launch page`

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}},

I will assume launch speed is not a priority this quarter and stop following up.

If it becomes one, reply “map” and I will send the two-minute walkthrough. No need to explain the silence.

{{sender_name|Alex}}
```

---

## Reply handling

| Reply | Action |
| --- | --- |
| “Send it” | Send the asset within 30 minutes, no pitch |
| “Not now” | Tag `later`, suppress for 90 days |
| “Who else do you work with?” | Send one relevant case study, ask one scoping question |
| “Unsubscribe” | Remove from all lists within 24 hours |
| Objection on price | Acknowledge, offer the small entry scope, do not discount the first call |
