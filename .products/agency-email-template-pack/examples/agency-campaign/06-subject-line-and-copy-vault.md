# 06. Subject Line & Copy Vault — Agency Services

## Cold outreach subject lines (2–5 words, human)

| Subject | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quick question about {{company}}` | Generic trigger | Lowest risk, highest reply on warm lists |
| `saw the new marketing hire` | Hiring trigger | Specific and timely |
| `{{first_name}} // one handoff question` | Founder-led | Personal, no hype |
| `the launch page problem` | Product launch | Names the pain directly |
| `41% faster launches` | Proof-led | Use only when the number is real |
| `bad time?` | Step 5 | Clean close, high reply rate |

## Warm and lifecycle subject lines

| Subject | Flow | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `the handoff map you asked for` | Welcome 1 | Deliver exactly what was promised |
| `one question before you decide` | Proposal 1 | Low-pressure objection surfacing |
| `your kickoff starts here` | Onboarding 1 | Clear next step |
| `first win, documented` | Onboarding 3 | Progress and one correction ask |
| `it has been a while — here is what changed` | Win-back 1 | No guilt, concrete updates |
| `the handoff nobody owns` | Newsletter | Curiosity plus practical value |

## Preview text pairings

| Subject | Preview text |
| --- | --- |
| `quick question about {{company}}` | One trigger, one question, no calendar link. |
| `saw the new marketing hire` | The bottleneck is usually not the hire. |
| `one question before you decide` | Budget timing, capacity, or scope? |
| `your kickoff starts here` | Access, owners, and the first 72 hours. |
| `it has been a while` | Three updates. No guilt. |

## Micro-commitment CTA bank

- “Worth a 2-minute look?”
- “Want me to send the map?”
- “Reply with A, B, or C.”
- “Should I send the short version?”
- “Open to a one-line reaction?”
- “Is this still on your radar?”

## Objection responses

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| “We already have an agency.” | “Good. This is a diagnostic map, not a pitch to replace them. Want it?” |
| “No budget this quarter.” | “Understood. I will send the map and check back when the next planning cycle starts.” |
| “Send more info.” | Ask one scoping question first: “Which is the bigger issue, launch speed or brand consistency?” |
| “Too expensive.” | “The entry scope is one workflow, not the full retainer. Want the one-page comparison?” |
| “Not the right person.” | “Who owns campaign launches on your side? I will send them the map directly.” |
