# 01. Audience, ICP & Trigger Research — Agency Services

**Reference campaign:** Northline Studio (branding + web for B2B SaaS)  
**Owner:** Founder / New Business Lead  
**Goal:** 12 qualified discovery conversations per month from a 900-contact warm and cold universe.

## Ideal Client Profile

| Dimension | Primary ICP | Disqualifiers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company size | 15–120 employees, $3M–$30M ARR | Pre-revenue or >500 employees |
| Buyer | Founder, CMO, VP Marketing, Head of Growth | Procurement-only gatekeepers |
| Trigger | New funding, product launch, rebrand, stalled pipeline, new marketing hire | No budget owner identifiable |
| Current state | In-house designer or freelancer, inconsistent brand system | Already has a 10-person brand team |
| Pain metric | 6–10 weeks to launch a campaign landing page | No active campaign calendar |

## Trigger Events (ranked by intent)

1. **New marketing leader hired in the last 60 days** — they need a visible early win.
2. **Product launch announced** — the site and deck usually lag behind the product.
3. **Funding round closed** — budget exists, but the brand still looks pre-seed.
4. **Hiring a designer or content marketer** — signals capacity strain, not a solved problem.
5. **Rebrand mentioned in a podcast or post** — public commitment creates urgency.

## Buyer language (from calls and public posts)

- “We look smaller than we are.”
- “Every campaign needs a new landing page and it takes three weeks.”
- “Our deck, site, and ads look like three different companies.”
- “I do not want a six-month brand project. I need something shipping this quarter.”

## Competitor teardown

| Competitor type | What they send | Gap to exploit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large brand agencies | Case studies with awards, 30-minute call CTA | Slow, expensive, no small entry scope |
| Freelance marketplaces | Price-led blasts, generic portfolios | No strategic diagnosis, high revision risk |
| Productized design services | Subscription pitch, unlimited requests | Weak brand thinking, queue-based delivery |

**White space:** a founder-led note that names the specific handoff slowing the team down, offers a small diagnostic asset, and asks for a reply rather than a meeting.

## Positioning for this campaign

> “We fix the one handoff between strategy and shipping that makes every campaign late.”

## List hygiene rules

- Remove role inboxes (`info@`, `hello@`) unless the buyer is a solo founder.
- Suppress anyone who received a cold touch in the last 45 days.
- Verify domains monthly; bounce rate must stay below 2%.
- Keep a separate suppression list for current clients and open proposals.
