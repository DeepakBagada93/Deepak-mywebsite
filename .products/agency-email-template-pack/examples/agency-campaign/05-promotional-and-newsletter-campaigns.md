# 05. Newsletter & Launch Campaigns

## Weekly editorial newsletter — “The Operator’s Edge”

**Cadence:** Thursday, 09:00 local time  
**Length:** 500–700 words, one idea, one example, one practical action  
**Structure:** Story → Lesson → Offer (SLO)

### Issue template

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | 2–5 words, curiosity without hype |
| Preheader | One sentence that adds context, not repetition |
| Story | A specific agency scene, told in 120 words |
| Lesson | The operating principle behind the scene |
| Example | One client result or a public teardown |
| Action | One 15-minute exercise the reader can run |
| Offer | A single soft CTA to the relevant service or asset |

### Example issue outline

- **Subject:** `the handoff nobody owns`
- **Story:** A four-person team with four campaigns queued and one designer.
- **Lesson:** Invisible handoffs survive because no single role is measured on them.
- **Example:** The component set that cut revision rounds from five to two.
- **Action:** List every handoff in the campaign path and name one owner.
- **CTA:** `Read the full teardown` (single link).

## 5-Day Launch Sequence

**Offer:** “Launch Sprint” — a two-week, fixed-scope engagement.

| Day | Subject | Angle | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `we found the launch bottleneck` | Curiosity + problem framing | Read the teardown |
| 2 | `launch sprint is open` | Official announcement, scope and price | See the scope |
| 3 | `41% faster launches, four screens` | Case study + proof | Read the case study |
| 4 | `two seats left this month` | Honest capacity notice, no fake scarcity | Claim a seat |
| 5 | `closing at 6pm` | Final call, clear deadline | Start the sprint |

### Promotional copy rules

- One primary CTA per email.
- No countdown timers, no fake urgency, no “act now”.
- Every claim links to a real case study or documented result.
- Send Day 5 to non-openers of Day 2–4 only, plus everyone who clicked but did not book.
- Remove anyone who bought the offer from the remaining sends.
