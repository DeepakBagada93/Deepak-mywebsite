# Agency Subject Line & Preview Text Vault

Every line below is written for a human inbox: 2–7 words, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no fake urgency. Pair each subject with a preview text that adds context instead of repeating the subject.

## Cold outreach

| Subject | Preview text | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quick question about {{company}}` | One trigger, one question, no calendar link. | Generic trigger with no specific hook |
| `saw the new marketing hire` | The bottleneck is usually not the hire. | New marketing leader in last 60 days |
| `{{first_name}} // one handoff question` | No pitch. Just one operating question. | Founder-led outbound |
| `the launch page problem` | Six weeks to ship one page is a handoff issue. | Product launch announced |
| `41% faster launches` | The before, the change, and the trade-offs. | Only when the number is documented |
| `bad time?` | I will close the loop either way. | Step 5 polite close |

| Subject | Preview text | Use when |

## Proposal & client

| Subject | Preview text | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `one question before you decide` | Budget timing, capacity, or scope? | 48 hours after proposal |
| `the smaller way to start` | Reduce scope, not the standard. | 5 days after proposal |
| `closing the file` | No guilt. The door stays open. | 10 days after proposal |
| `your kickoff starts here` | Access, owners, and the first 72 hours. | After signature |
| `first win, documented` | One result and one correction ask. | Day 7 of onboarding |
| `it has been a while — here is what changed` | Three updates. No guilt. | 60–90 day win-back |
| `know one team with this problem?` | A 15-second ask, no follow-up. | After a visible win |

## Rules

1. Never use the same subject for two touches in one thread; use `Re:` for follow-ups.
2. Keep preview text under 90 characters.
3. Do not put the offer name and the deadline in the same subject line.
4. Test one variable at a time: trigger vs. outcome, or question vs. statement.
5. Retire any subject that gets replies but no qualified conversations after 100 sends.
