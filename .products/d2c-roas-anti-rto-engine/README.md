## ⚡ What is the Indian D2C ROAS & Anti-RTO Engine?

In the Indian e-commerce landscape, two brutal factors determine whether an e-commerce brand survives:
1. **Ad Creative Fatigue**: Scaling Meta Ads requires producing 10–20 fresh, authentic Hinglish video creatives every single week.
2. **The COD RTO Trap**: 65%–75% of Indian e-commerce orders are Cash-on-Delivery. In Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, 25% to 40% of these orders get returned to origin (RTO) due to fake addresses, impulsive cancellations, or buyer ghosting—wasting forward and reverse courier freight (₹150–₹220 per failed delivery).

The **Indian D2C ROAS & Anti-RTO Engine** solves both sides of this equation:
- **Creative Generator**: Produces authentic, high-converting Hinglish UGC scripts, Reels hooks, and direct-response ad copy.
- **Address Hygiene & Bogus Order Filter**: Automatically scans incoming addresses to flag missing house numbers or fake pincodes.
- **WhatsApp 2-Touch COD Confirmation**: Engages the customer via WhatsApp to confirm delivery intent and incentivizes instant UPI prepayment with a ₹50 discount.
- **Post-Delivery Review & Repeat Retention**: Auto-solicits WhatsApp reviews and delivers personalized repeat purchase vouchers.
