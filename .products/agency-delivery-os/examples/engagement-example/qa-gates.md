# QA gates — Quote Automation

## Gate 1 — Success metric

- The agreed metric is demonstrable: Cut manual quote turnaround from two days to under four hours for repeat customers.
- Evidence is recorded in the handoff, not asserted verbally.

## Gate 2 — Deliverables

- Deliverable 1: Intake form with validation — reviewed by Priya Raman.
- Deliverable 2: Quote calculation service — reviewed by Priya Raman.
- Deliverable 3: Internal review dashboard — reviewed by Priya Raman.

## Gate 3 — Quality

- Build passes from a clean checkout.
- Tests pass; no skipped or deleted tests.
- No secrets or client data in the repository or in prompts.

## Gate 4 — Handoff

- Owner, runbook, and rollback steps are written down.
- Open items list is empty or explicitly accepted by the client.
